Space words explained

DSN: Deep Space Network, NASA's worldwide radio antenna network used to communicate with distant spacecraft.

Sync pattern: A known sequence of bits that helps a receiver identify data boundaries.

Bit: A binary digit representing either 0 or 1.

Encoding: Representing information as digital data for storage or transmission.

Signal detection: Identifying a spacecraft's radio signal among received signals.

Error correction: Recovering information that may have been damaged during transmission.

Majority voting: A simplified error-correction method that compares three copies of a bit and selects the value appearing at least twice.

Mission score: A game performance score based on mistakes made during the mission. It is not an actual NASA mission success metric.

ABOUT THIS SIMULATION

This game introduces the basic principles of deep-space communication through simplified interactive activities.

Signal sounds, synchronization timing, and error-correction puzzles are educational models, not exact reproductions of NASA DSN operations.
